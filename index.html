KeyChess

Think Type Take

A chess puzzle trainer made to improve your focus. You don't drag pieces: you look at the position, think, and type the whole line, your moves and the opponent's replies. To type it, you have to calculate it first.

How it works

  1. Choose the number of puzzles, the rating and the theme. The session starts at once.
  2. The app plays the first move. The chip at the top shows how many moves to type.
  3. Type with the on-screen keys or your keyboard. Space enters a move. To castle, move the king two squares (Kg1 or Kc1). Press Check.
  4. Missed puzzles go to Review. Replay a session in Training to add cycles. Stats shows your progress.

Errors: the switch above the board. On, a move turns red as soon as you press Space if it is illegal or not in the solution. Off, nothing turns red, which is harder. The flag sends us a report about the puzzle.

No account, no ads. Your progress stays on your device.
